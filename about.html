Our Story

Established in 2009

Stitches For Bounces LLC has been repairing commercial inflatables out of Ocean County, New Jersey since 2009. What started with one owner-operator and a single industrial sewing machine has grown into a full-time repair shop that party rental companies across the country rely on to keep their bounce houses, water slides and obstacle courses in service.

Most of our work comes from rental businesses, not individual homeowners — operators who can't afford downtime and need repairs done right the first time. We travel on-site for customers within reach of Toms River, and we accept shipped-in units from anywhere in the country. You can see recent jobs, before and after, on our Our Work page.

We're also an authorized warranty repair provider for several major inflatable manufacturers, including Magic Jump, Inflatable Depot, EZ Inflatables, JumpOrange, Gorilla Bounce, Bag Jump, Vertical Reality and Moonwalk USA, among others. If your unit is still under factory warranty, ask us — there's a good chance we can handle the repair directly.

In order to proceed with repairs, every inflatable must be dry and clean. If we have to clean and dry the inflatable, a fee will be charged at an hourly rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long has Stitches For Bounces been in business?
We've been repairing commercial inflatables since 2009 — over 17 years, and more than 10,000 units repaired for rental companies across the country.
Do you travel to my state, or do I need to ship my unit?
Both. We travel on-site for NJ, NY, PA, MD, DE, CT and MA, and we accept shipped-in units for shop repairs from anywhere in the United States.
Do you handle factory warranty repairs?
Yes. We're an authorized warranty repair provider for several major manufacturers, including Magic Jump, Inflatable Depot, EZ Inflatables, JumpOrange, Gorilla Bounce, Bag Jump, Vertical Reality and Moonwalk USA. Contact us with your brand and we'll confirm.
Do you work with individual owners, or only rental companies?
Most of our work is for party rental companies that need fast turnaround to keep equipment in rotation. We do also take on individual owners' repairs — reach out and we'll let you know if it's a good fit.
