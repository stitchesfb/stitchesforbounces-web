Our Story

Established in 2009

Established in 2009, Stitches for Bounces LLC has been a trusted name in the entertainment industry for inflatable repair services, catering to clients across the United States. With a dedicated focus on bounce house repair, our skilled technicians ensure thorough and efficient repairs, whether it's sewing repair, slide covers, or broken parts. We pride ourselves on delivering prompt and reliable service to meet the needs of our valued American customers.

From minor touch-ups to major repairs, our team utilizes advanced techniques and quality materials to ensure durable and long-lasting results. Located in Ocean County, New Jersey, we serve customers across the United States, offering convenient repair options tailored to their needs. Experience peace of mind knowing that your bounce houses are in expert hands with Stitches For Bounces LLC.

In order to proceed with repairs, every inflatable must be dry and clean. If we have to clean and dry the inflatable, a fee will be charged at an hourly rate.
