Mesh Window Replacement

Torn mesh windows are one of the most common repairs we see on rental bounce houses. We replace damaged or torn mesh and netting on windows and entryways with matching commercial-grade mesh, sewn in to restore visibility, airflow and safety.

  • Torn or damaged mesh window replacement
  • Entryway and door netting replacement
  • Safety netting added to slides
  • Matching commercial-grade mesh and trim
  • Reinforced stitching to prevent re-tearing
  • Window netting repair on jumpers and combos
