Frequently Asked Questions

Please reach us at guevara@stitchesforbounces.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

How long until my inflatable is ready?
Typically, our bounce house repairs are efficiently handled within a few hours, ensuring minimal disruption to your plans. Our skilled technicians strive for same-day completion whenever possible, prioritizing prompt service without compromising on quality.
How much does bounce house repair cost?
The pricing for bounce house repair is determined by factors such as the severity of damage and required fixes. Our transparent pricing model ensures fair estimates. Contact us today for a complimentary assessment and personalized quote tailored to your bounce house repair needs.
Do you travel to the location of the bounce house?
Yes! While our headquarters are in New Jersey, we offer mobile service in nearby states to repair your bounce house on-site. Contact us to schedule a visit and bring the fun back to your event. We also accept nationwide shipping for inflatable repairs.
